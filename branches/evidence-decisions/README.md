# Evidence and decisions

This branch preserves the author's source-based work on evidence, consent, applicability and decision standards. Psychiatric, pharmacological and legal reviews are part of the wider research history.

The connection is the operational question: which evidence warrants a particular reading or action? A full normalized-positive UHL model is not established for every historical domain review.

## Record and scope

A decision record retains the source, proposition, applicable context, outcome, consent and relevant version. Domain rules supply the admissible inference and decision operations. Jurisdiction and applicability belong to the record where legal propositions are used.

The existing [historical claim catalogue](../../registry/atlas.csv) records some of these works outside the earlier C1–C4 composition conditions. That is a statement about those conditions, distinct from an empirical refutation.

The [public repository inventory](../../registry/repositories.json) points to preserved source repositories. Listing a work records its provenance; it does not establish every conclusion in it. A future mathematical extension supplies the common [branch contract](../../docs/branch-contract.md) and the missing domain mapping.
