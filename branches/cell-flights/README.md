# Observatory cell-response flights

The GitHub Observatory's Flight 01 and Flight 02 are the earlier cellular-response laboratory trials. They have their own frozen models, held-out comparisons, baselines and success gates.

They are distinct from the [Arc Virtual Cell Challenge](../vcc/README.md), its genetic-perturbation predictor and official competition entries.

| Trial | Result | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flight 01 | The added candidate failed its declared success gate | [Frozen experiment](https://github.com/thantiklermcirony/empirical-observatory/tree/main/research/virtual-cell) |
| Flight 02 | Retained error improved by 0.13%, below the 10% gate | [Frozen experiment](https://github.com/thantiklermcirony/empirical-observatory/tree/main/research/virtual-cell-flight02) |

## Operational relationship

The trials ask whether additional retained state improves a specified future cellular-response reading. Their observation conventions, inputs, model and assay endpoint belong to the frozen experiments. Their relationship to the programme is that declared state-and-prediction interface.

An added candidate's failure is recorded with its comparison. It does not stand in for an Arc competition result. Conversely, a competition score does not retrospectively change a flight's gate.
