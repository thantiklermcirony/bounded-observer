The capstone

Bounded Composition and Its Horizons: when a bounded quantity earns an additive chart, a horizon and a geometry. Version 2.1, 30 September 2026, 57 pages. Its claim register has 38 entries: 12 proven, 11 derived, 15 open. The Universal Hyperbolic Law is its central theorem.

Ready for specialist review in four areas: functional equations and ordered structures; stochastic boundaries; cone and Hilbert geometry; and the drug-combination analysis. Then Journal of Mathematical Psychology, the home of the Luce–Marley–Narens lineage the result sits in.

In review

Finite rescue windows and supply-limited redox commitment in NRF2-active cancer. Major revision. Its second revision reports a negative result about its own earlier proposal: the experiment as designed could not have distinguished the model from a simple power law even if the model were exactly correct. The design was rebuilt around measurements made below the threshold, which commit no cells, linked to the passage experiment by an identity in which the rate constant cancels exactly.

The parent

The Observer and the World: predictive closure, the projective shadow, and a first-principles grammar of empirical science — SSRN 7347861. The stance the whole programme is read through. To be revised to point down to the capstone once the capstone's readers report.

Not yet written
