One project. A common operational foundation.

The Bounded Observer studies what an observer can record, read, retain, combine and change. UHL gives the operational mathematics of bounded normalized readings. SIH studies regularity and concentration in specified field models. The branches turn these questions into models, instruments and experiments.

What is closed?

[P, conditional] In the finite complex record model, the declared readings, instruments, composition, purification, environment dilation and discarding operations stay within the record class. The closure theorem has named premises. Applications identify their own records, dynamics and observation bridges.

The general predictive-state question and the independent two-positive-weight model also support classical branches. A shared interface does not impose a quantum interpretation on a cell assay or an AI record.
