The six-gate reading map

The broader work belongs at the gate where its claim is tested. A paper can contribute to more than one gate, and a connection is not a transfer of evidence between domains.

Reading limits

What the receipts establish

The 41 records include earlier versions and related papers. The 41 research families and 41 original numbered items overlap; they are not 82 independent discoveries. The 471 indexed main-text pages are a retrieval aid, not proof that every possible prediction was found. A source hash identifies the supplied PDF bytes. It does not verify proofs, novelty or the latest online revision.

The complete original inventory includes author manuscript passages and keeps its original publication terms. This site's compact derivative deliberately omits those passages. The individual papers and third-party sources retain their own rights; the repository's general text licence does not relicense original manuscripts.
