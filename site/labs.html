Completed · success gate failed

Recovery Lab

In 214 older female mice, 3,445 of 4,104 outcomes were resolvable (83.94%), below the 90% prerequisite. Added recovery history cut error by 0.84%, below the declared 5% gate, and won in three of five folds. The result does not demonstrate longer life or causal rejuvenation.

Completed · success gate failed

Virtual Cell

Two held-out flights tested whether an added control profile improves prediction. Flight 02 covered 50 cell lines and 92 exact drug/dose identities; retained error improved only 0.13% against a 10% gate, winning one of five folds. Both candidates failed their declared success gates.

What changes next

What the previous tests require

Recovery: establish the future outcome and a resolvable cohort before evaluating added history again; the earlier 83.94% outcome resolution missed its own admission gate. Virtual Cell: choose an independent response set and compare a predeclared representation with stronger baselines before crediting added control-profile information. Cancer Lab: measure the proposed below-threshold state and the later passage in the same experimental preparation before claiming a finite rescue mechanism. IDA: require an independently measured return dynamic and matched feedback comparison.

These are next-test directions, not claims that the tests have run. Each source package retains its full protocol and revisions.

Reading limits

A laboratory is not a verdict on every claim

These results are scoped to the stated datasets, interventions and software versions. The failed gates remain failures. A synthetic demonstration tests code inside its model; a source hash identifies bytes; a prospective mechanism needs its own calibration and independent comparison. The research library retains the wider paper and prediction record without promoting historical author claims into new validation.
