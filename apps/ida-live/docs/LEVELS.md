# Levels (v0.4)

Six scenes. Each one invites the state it measures, and the image answers in the same language.
Every level is a test first: a settle phase (just watching) sets your baseline **in that scene**,
then three rounds, one of them a sealed replay of an earlier round (the sham). You rate each round.

| # | Level | State | Signal (engine feature) | The scene answers by |
|---|-------|-------|-------------------------|----------------------|
| 1 | Still Water | calm, restful alertness | relative alpha, delta excluded (`ix_alpha_rel3`) | wind ripples fade, mist lifts; a drop is the knock |
| 2 | Bloom | warmth, approach | frontal alpha asymmetry ln αAF8 − ln αAF7 (`ix_faa`) | the flower opens and warms; a chill is the knock |
| 3 | Tightrope | steady focus | engagement β/(α+θ) (`ix_engagement`), gated by stability | the rope stops swaying, the walker crosses; a gust |
| 4 | Ride | flow | frontal theta share (`ix_theta_af_rel`) with engagement inside a band | the wave carries you (speed); too hard blurs, too little stalls |
| 5 | Storm | recovery | distance from your settle state in (relative alpha, engagement) | the sea slows as you return; thunder is the knock, recovery is timed |
| 6 | Night Sky | open awareness (experimental) | Lempel-Ziv complexity (`lzc`) | the view widens and stars come up; a meteor pulls attention |

## Units
`z = (fast index − its settle mean) × (engine spread / fast spread) ÷ reference scale`.
Zero is your baseline in the scene (watching video lowers alpha, so a calibration zero made
Bloom v0.3 near impossible: your settle sat 1.3 SD below the calibration). The scale is your
frozen reference, so tiers are comparable across days. `z_ref` (zero at the calibration) is
recorded alongside. A reference made before v0.4 lacks the new features: that level then uses
your settle spread as the scale until you recalibrate.

## Clean signal
Blinks are detected live on the forehead sensors and *held out* (the index freezes for 0.5 s)
instead of counting as lost signal. Contact, movement and muscle still count as lost signal,
and the scene says so after 2.5 s ("head movement: rest your head and stay still").

## Ladder
Nine fixed tiers per level, generated by `tools/make_levels.py`. Ascend: 70% of 12 s in the
corridor; descend: under 20%. Knocks start at tier 2 (Storm: from tier 1) and get stronger
and more frequent up the ladder. Top tier is Monk.

## v0.5
* **Sound** (web/js/audio.js): every TAO world variable is also heard. q opens a low-pass on the
  bed and chord; the chord's voices beat against each other out of the corridor and lock into a
  pure chord in it; h is a breath pacer (6/min) plus a felt low pulse on the inhale (bone
  conduction), stronger as your breathing locks to it; n sets sparkle density; each knock has its
  own sound; load thins the world. Master limiter and a hard ceiling (Sound tab).
* **Rhythmic tone, sealed**: per round, pulses at the level's target rhythm (10 Hz calm, 6 Hz flow,
  18 Hz focus, 40 Hz open awareness) or the same pulses at irregular intervals. Revealed at the
  end; the report compares them.
* **Breathing** is read from the head's own motion (gyroscope), with its lock to the pacer.
* **Chamber follows the TAO contracts** (see SPEC_AUDIT.md). The v0.4 critical-slowing term that
  brightened the image, and so rewarded muscle strain, is gone.
* **Skip** (button, S or →): intro, settle (once it has a usable baseline), round, rating.
* **Attention map** (docs/ATTENTION.md) and personal signatures for Tightrope and Night Sky.
* **Reports**: Levels → What the levels did; each level's end card → What did my brain do?

## v0.6: music and feeling
* **Adaptive orchestral score** (web/js/music.js, Tone.js + VSCO 2 CE samples, CC0): each level has
  its own key, mode and theme (Still Water D major, serene; Bloom F major, rising; Tightrope A minor
  turning major; Ride E Mixolydian, driving; Storm D minor to D major; Night Sky C Lydian, awe).
  Off target the harmony waits on suspended and dominant chords and the melody rests; in the
  corridor it resolves on the next strong beat; each tier adds an instrument on a bar line; a
  climb is a peak (swell, cymbal, cadence, the theme with a leaning note); a knock displaces the
  harmony and each bar in the corridor walks it home; bars are sized so one breath is a whole
  number of bars and the phrase rises on the inhale. See docs/EXPERIENCE.md.
* **Hear it without the headband**: Sound tab → Hear a level's music (a 95 s guided preview).
* **How it felt**: an affect grid (pleasant–unpleasant by calm–energised) before the level, after
  each round and at the end; press C for a chill. The report tests whether chills fall on the
  music's designed peaks more than chance, and tracks mood before vs after across sessions.
* **Fun**: a light swell on each climb, personal bests per level (best tier, fastest return).
