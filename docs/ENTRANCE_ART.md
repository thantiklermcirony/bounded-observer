# Entrance artwork

`site/observer-hero.png` was generated with the built-in image-generation tool on
1 October 2026 for the website and GitHub profile. It is an illustration, not a scientific
diagram or evidence for any claim.

## Prompt

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide editorial entrance artwork for the GitHub
> profile and hero of a research website. Primary request: visualize “The Bounded Observer —
> science from within.” Show the viewer located inside a finite circular field of observation:
> a tiny human-scale observer mark near the center, a few measured points and paths nearby,
> and fine curved geodesic and branching lines that grow denser toward a distant luminous
> horizon that never becomes a hard wall. Let the structure convey limited access, predictive
> state, bounded composition and the difference between local measurements and a larger
> unseen world. Style: sophisticated scientific editorial illustration, restrained pen-and-ink
> line work with subtle paper grain and translucent washes, contemporary rather than fantasy
> or photorealism. Palette: warm off-white #fbfaf8, near-black charcoal #1b1a18, muted copper
> #b4552b, very sparing slate blue #2b6b8f. Wide landscape composition suitable for a 2.4:1
> banner crop, with generous quiet space around the focal structure and clean margins. No
> text, letters, numerals, equations, logos, telescope, observatory building, computer,
> medical imagery, planets, stars, or decorative clutter. The image is illustrative art,
> not a scientific diagram. Crisp enough to read when reduced to a GitHub README banner.

The site keeps all actual wording in HTML, so it stays legible and accessible at different
screen sizes. The image's alternative text describes its concept without treating it as a
mathematical result.
