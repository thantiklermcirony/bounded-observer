# Research tasks

The board records specific questions that follow from the current library and instruments. It is a set of next tasks, not a claim that new experiments have run. Each empirical task needs its power, error bands and loss condition fixed before the decisive data.

| ID | Question and source | Entry point | What settles the task |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M1 | Global regularity and persistence of the interior margin for the pressure-coupled Klein fluid — Paper 5 §9, Conjecture 12 | Equations (9.1)–(9.2), a stated function space and controlled initial data | A proof, or a rigorous admissible counterexample losing margin or regularity in finite time; numerical searches are evidence for candidates |
| M2 | A specified non-equilibrium gravity completion — Paper 7 §8.2 | The four listed closure/specification requirements | A constructed, checked model or an obstruction under named assumptions |
| M3 | For one named physical coupling, a response/action bridge or same-fibre countermodel — Paper 8 Theorem 6 | Independently specified model family, preparation data and readout | Factorization is proved, or admissible completions with the same data and different coupling are exhibited |
| M4 | A physical selection principle within the locally covariant rate cone — Paper 8 Theorem 5 | [Live rate-cone operations](../toolkit/RECORDS.md) and a separately named physical principle | A selector is derived, or a countermodel meets the proposed principle without the claimed selection |
| E1 | Two declared diagonal interventions translate log odds and commute — Paper 2 Corollary 3 | A frozen two-outcome intervention protocol that establishes the model premises | Observations agree within the fixed error/power criterion, or slope/order effects reject the stated physical model |
| E2 | Curvature equals Var_A minus Var_B — Paper 2 Theorem 4 | Independent clonal/rate measurements and a declared clock | An independently measured curvature matches the two variance terms, or fails the fixed comparison; cancellation and clock changes are controlled |
| E3 | Filter order angle equals the specified polar rotation — Paper 1 Theorem 5 | Equilibrium input, calibrated canonical positive noncommuting qubit/polarization filters and the declared triangle | The measured angle agrees with Ω, or differs beyond the fixed error criterion; arbitrary initial states and general updates are outside this identity |
| E4 | Matched-endpoint hormesis prediction — amendment §6 | Independently measured components, baseline and endpoint; [live response models](../toolkit/HORMESIS.md) | Held-out onset, peak or both assay crossings meet the preregistered bands or reject the selected model |
| I1 | IDA independent read gate — historical H5 and current IDA contract | Matched present, common future, independent behavior and held sessions versus a fixed comparator | The predeclared held-out improvement gate passes or fails |
| A1 | Warrant-preserving memory improves useful work — Scientific AI branch | Frozen source-change workloads with independently assessed consequential errors | The fixed correctness/work-saved gate passes or fails |
| C1 | Advance the Arc predictor beyond its recorded control | Whole-context/target holdouts, fixed count renderer and official validation receipts | The candidate meets its declared biological promotion gate and receives its own challenge result |
| C2 | Prepare an Arc source release | Challenge reuse terms, dataset/code licences, sanitized dependencies and a reproduction interface | A lawful reproducible release is constructed; no private credentials, unpublished data or submission secrets are included |

The historical T15 and T15b analyses **failed their registered criteria**. E2 is a new, independently specified measurement task; it does not relabel those results as inconclusive. Numerical and empirical tests do not prove a general PDE or mathematical theorem.
