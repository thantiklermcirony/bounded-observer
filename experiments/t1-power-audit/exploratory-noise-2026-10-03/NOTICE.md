# Third-party material

The outputs in this folder are derived from the DECREASE validation data:
Ianevski A. et al., DECREASE, https://github.com/IanevskiAleksandr/DECREASE, at commit
`0f5871cad64fb8c43a88242e02baac175ed98d0e`. That repository is licensed under the GNU General
Public License v3.0.

Most columns are derived statistics. The `v00` column of `noise_forensic.csv` copies one value per
block verbatim from the DECREASE files (the response in the untreated (0,0) well). It is
redistributed under the terms of the GPL-3.0, with this attribution. No other DECREASE file is
included.
