# Reading and source guide

These sources support the scientific and historical anchors. The family scenes in the book and the hypothetical encounters in the essays are literary constructions. Philosophical interpretations and ethical commitments are argued in the prose.

The current programme papers are author manuscripts. Their mathematical results have stated premises; their empirical histories retain failed and exploratory outcomes. The sources below do not turn those manuscripts into independent empirical confirmation.

## Essay 1 — You Were Not There at the Beginning

**[Learning-induced neural plasticity of speech processing before birth](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3773755/).** Eino Partanen and colleagues. A repeated prenatal sound exposure was associated with stronger neonatal neural responses to specified sound-feature changes.

**[Where Your Elements Came From](https://science.nasa.gov/image-article/apod-2017-october-24-where-your-elements-came-from/).** NASA Astronomy Picture of the Day. Carbon and oxygen in bodies have stellar formation histories; much bodily hydrogen derives from the early universe.

**[How the Eyes Work](https://www.nei.nih.gov/eye-health-information/healthy-vision/how-eyes-work).** National Eye Institute. Seeing depends on physical light reception and signal transduction rather than an inner person viewing a literal miniature object.

**[Mechanism of phototaxis in marine zooplankton](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature07590).** Gáspár Jékely and colleagues. A simple eyespot can help guide swimming through its local coupling to ciliary activity without forming an image.

**[Temporal comparisons in bacterial chemotaxis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC387059/).** J. E. Segall, S. M. Block and H. C. Berg. Recent chemical stimulation influences bacterial response and adaptation machinery affects that temporal comparison.

## Essay 2 — The Body You Keep Becoming

**[Whatever Does Not Kill Us: Cumulative Lifetime Adversity, Vulnerability, and Resilience](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/4b6787gk).** Mark D. Seery, E. Alison Holman and Roxane Cohen Silver. In the studied sample, some reported prior adversity was associated with better selected outcomes than either no or high reported adversity.

**[Exact Onset and Peak Laws for Bounded Adaptive Responses](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7603323).** Daniel John Murray. A beneficial interval requires an actual repair advantage relative to damage under the chosen model and endpoint; earlier activation alone does not guarantee it.

**[Hallmarks of aging: An expanding universe](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36599349/).** Carlos López-Otín, Maria A. Blasco, Linda Partridge, Manuel Serrano and Guido Kroemer. Ageing research examines interrelated genetic damage, protein maintenance, mitochondrial function, stem-cell capacity and intercellular communication among other processes.

**[The dual process model of coping with bereavement: Rationale and description](https://doi.org/10.1080/074811899201046).** Margaret Stroebe and Henk Schut. An influential model describes shifts between attending to a loss and attending to changed-life tasks rather than demanding uninterrupted grief confrontation.

## Essay 3 — The Past Arrives Through Other People

**[Narrative of the Life of Frederick Douglass, an American Slave](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/23/23-h/23-h.htm).** Frederick Douglass. Sophia Auld began teaching Douglass letters, Hugh Auld stopped instruction; Douglass continued learning including from boys in Baltimore and described the painful difference between understanding his condition and obtaining freedom.

**[Reconstruction of Automobile Destruction: An Example of the Interaction Between Language and Memory](https://gwern.net/doc/psychology/cognitive-bias/1974-loftus.pdf).** Elizabeth F. Loftus and John C. Palmer. Question wording affected reported collision speeds; in experiment II the smashed wording produced more broken-glass reports at one week than hit wording, despite absence of glass.

**[Report of the Royal Commission into the Robodebt Scheme](https://www.royalcommission.gov.au/robodebt/report).** Royal Commission into the Robodebt Scheme, Commissioner Catherine Holmes. Income averaging without other evidence was an unreliable basis for entitlement/debt conclusions; recipients were treated as receiving income at times they had not, with legal and institutional failures and unreasonable burdens of producing historical records.

**[The Bounded Observer, and The Ceiling and the Constants](https://github.com/thantiklermcirony/bounded-observer).** Daniel John Murray. Retention adequacy depends on the questions and operations supported. A summary that merges histories with different admitted future readings is insufficient for that declared task.

## Essay 4 — The Room to Be Wrong

The author has written affectionately and satirically about the character's confidence. This essay develops the distinction between confidence to attempt and evidence-sensitive correction without reproducing mock statistical claims.

**[The Bounded Observer and current project operational definitions](https://github.com/thantiklermcirony/bounded-observer).** Daniel John Murray. A retained description must preserve history distinctions that change the admitted future readings or actions it is intended to support.

**[On the Mode of Communication of Cholera, second edition](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/72894/72894-h/72894-h.htm).** John Snow. Snow investigated water usage and illness distribution; the handle was removed, but he recorded that attacks had declined beforehand and could not establish whether the well remained infectious at removal.

**[Exact Onset and Peak Laws for Bounded Adaptive Responses; proposed hormesis correction](https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=7603323).** Daniel John Murray. Boundedness alone does not force a beneficial interval; corrected onset/peak conclusions require the chosen repair/harm model and endpoint assumptions.

**[Cargo Cult Science](https://calteches.library.caltech.edu/51/2/CargoCult.htm).** Richard P. Feynman; Caltech commencement address. Feynman urged scientists to guard against self-deception and disclose weaknesses or contrary information instead of presenting only the favorable account.
